How to fill in your mappings

A quick guide to completing your assigned source field mappings on your phone or computer.

✓
Your progress is saved automatically. Every time you tap Save, your answer is stored. You can close the app, come back later, and pick up exactly where you left off — just use the same username.
Getting started
1

Open the app

Open the Concept Mapper link on your phone or computer. You don't need to install anything.

2

Enter your username

Type in the username you've been given and tap Continue. This is how the app knows which mappings belong to you.

3

Pick a source field

You'll see a list of all source fields. Tap any one to start filling it in. Fields you've already completed show a ✓.

4

Fill in the four fields and save

Enter the Domain ID, Name, Concept ID, and Vocabulary for that source field, then tap Save mapping. The app will move to the next field automatically.

What each field means
Domain ID
The OMOP domain this concept belongs to. For example: Condition, Drug, Measurement, Observation, Procedure.
Name
The standard concept name as it appears in the OMOP vocabulary. This is the human-readable label for the concept.
Concept ID
The unique numeric ID for this concept in the OMOP vocabulary. For example: 4329847.
Vocab
The vocabulary the concept comes from. For example: SNOMED, LOINC, RxNorm, ICD10CM.
Your username

Use the username you've been assigned — for example jane_smith or reviewer_2.

Always use the same username each time you open the app. If you use a different name, your previous work won't appear and you'll create a separate set of mappings.

If you're unsure what your username should be, check with the person who sent you this link.

Tips
↩ You can come back anytime. Close the app whenever you need to — your saved mappings are stored and will be there when you return.
✎ You can edit a mapping you've already saved — just tap the field in the list, make your changes, and tap Save again.
↕ Use the Previous and Next buttons to move between fields without going back to the list.
◎ The progress bar at the top shows how many fields you've completed out of the total.
